Only you can open your vault

Your vault is encrypted on your device before it's saved or synced.

  • AES-256-GCM encryption, with a key made from your master password (scrypt).
  • End-to-end: your master password never leaves your devices, so nobody else, including us, can read your vault.
  • Prefer no password? Unlock with your Jace account, or let a device remember it. You choose, and the app tells you the trade-off.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking. Breach checks send only part of a hash, never a password.
  • Every line of code is public on GitHub.
